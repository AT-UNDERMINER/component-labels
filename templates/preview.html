Label template preview

Each label is exactly 45×45mm, shown at 3× scale. Sample data is hardcoded in this page; the real templates are Jinja2 (label_simple / label_complex / label_connector) rendered by label_builder.py. Layout CSS is shared via label_styles.css, so the geometry shown here is exactly what WeasyPrint prints.

Information hierarchy: the headline (largest text) is the most useful identifier for the part — not always the MPN. Passives lead with their value; actives, LEDs and connectors lead with the MPN plus a short descriptor.

Value is the headline — MPN is secondary (resistor, ceramic / electrolytic cap, zener, inductor)
